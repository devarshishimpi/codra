# Getting Support

Welcome! Here are the best ways to get help with Codra.

---

## Ask a question

The fastest way to get an answer is to join the community Discord. Post in the appropriate channel and someone from the team or the community will usually respond quickly.

**Discord:** https://discord.gg/qkJmc8hHrC

---

## Report a bug

If you've found a bug or unexpected behaviour, please open a GitHub issue. Include steps to reproduce, relevant logs or error messages, and your runtime (Cloudflare Workers, Node.js, or Docker) and version.

**GitHub Issues:** https://github.com/devarshishimpi/codra/issues

---

## Community chat

For longer discussions, feature ideas, or questions that don't fit neatly into a bug report, use GitHub Discussions or the Discord community. Both are monitored by maintainers.

- **Discussions:** https://github.com/devarshishimpi/codra/discussions
- **Discord:** https://discord.gg/qkJmc8hHrC
